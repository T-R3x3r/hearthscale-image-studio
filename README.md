# Image studio

Make and change pictures with image models on your own device.

Write what you want to see and choose **Make**. The picture is made on your
device, or on a Hearthscale Server you paired with it, and stays in your
library. Your words and pictures go only to your own Hearthscale Servers.

## What you can do

- **Make** a picture from a few words, in the shape and size you pick, with
  an optional style such as Photo, Watercolour or Pixel art.
- **Vary** a picture a little or a lot, or make **more** like it.
- **Change the picture** you see: say what to change, such as the season or
  the light.
- **Mark** parts of the picture with the brush or a box, in up to four
  colours. Say what goes in each colour, or leave it empty to remove what is
  there. One press changes each area in turn, and every pixel outside the
  marks stays as it was. **Invert** marks everything that is not marked, and
  **Soft edge** blends each change into the picture.
- **Quick changes**, with a model made for editing: **Remove all text**,
  **Remove the interface** of a game or an app, **Make it night** and
  **Remove the background**, each in one press. With such a model, you can
  also add pictures to the prompt or to one marked area, such as a photo of
  a shirt for a person you marked.
- **Extend** a picture past its edges.
- **Before and after**: slide a line across a changed picture to see how it
  was before.
- **Enlarge** a picture into a larger, sharper copy.
- **Compare** two to four models on the same words, side by side.
- Keep favourites, search your library, and save or copy any picture.

Your pictures stay on your device, in Image studio's own folder.

## What you need

- **Hearthscale**, with a Server on this device or on a device you paired.
- **An image model.** The first time you open Image studio, the introduction
  offers the image model recommended for your device. To use another model,
  open the model picker under the prompt. The picker lists the models on your
  Servers, and **Get more models** opens the Server, where you can find and
  download more.
- **An upscale model** for Enlarge. When there is none, Enlarge offers the
  model recommended for your device.
- **A chat model** is optional. With one, you can improve your words and
  describe a picture.

## Price

Image studio is free. It needs no account.
